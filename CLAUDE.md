# Slate: notes for making changes

- The whole app is `src/slate.html`. Edit that file only; `dist/` is generated by `python3 tools/build.py` and isn't committed.
- `src/slate.html` is also published as a Claude artifact. Keep it artifact-safe: no `<html>/<head>/<body>` tags, `<title>` and `<style>` at the top, no external hosts except Google Fonts and the allowed CDNs.
- **Never break saved data.** Users' data is in localStorage under `slate.v1` (tasks per `YYYY-MM-DD`, plus `__routines`, `__ideas`, `__feelings`, `__categories`, `__kids`, `__seeded`) and `slate.avatar`, `slate.theme`, `slate.style`, `slate.accents`, `slate.colors`, `slate.cal`, `slate.view`. Don't rename or reset these keys. If the shape must change, add a migration that reads the old shape and upgrades it in place.
- Don't reseed example data: seeding is guarded by `store.__seeded` and must stay that way.
- Accounts and sync use Supabase project `vdryzddjkvalxjbmfshh` (table `public.slate_data`, one row per user, row-level security on). Password help uses `public.account_recovery` (bcrypt-hashed answers) and sign-in lockout uses `public.signin_guard`; clients reach both only through the `set_recovery_answers`, `has_recovery_answers`, `reset_password_with_answers`, `signin_failed` and `signin_succeeded` functions. Username accounts sign in with `<original name>@users.imatiny408-tech.github.io` (Supabase rejects example.com as an invalid email; the old `@slate.example.com` is still recognised); the current, changeable username lives in `public.profiles` and `login_email_for` maps it back. Chores are sent with `send_family_chores` into `public.chore_inbox`; the kid picks them up with `fetch_family_chores_v2` (each sender has an opaque `src`, and a sender's newest list replaces only their own earlier chores) and `ack_chores`; the family reads what was sent with `family_lists`. `create_username_account` makes username accounts server-side (no email step). Passkeys use Supabase Auth's built-in passkey endpoints (`/auth/v1/passkeys/...`). The app talks to it with plain `fetch` and the publishable key. `slate.session` and `slate.sync` in localStorage hold the sign-in and the last-synced marker; they are not part of backups. Sync is off inside the Claude preview.
- Pushing to `main` deploys to GitHub Pages automatically.
- The Next view (`__next` in `slate.v1`: `steps`, plus per-date `done`/`skip`/`focus` and `place`) is the routine queue. Don't rename it; `nextData()` fills missing fields in place. Location is a manual Home/Work/Out switch; the queue orders steps by routine, clock, today's timed tasks and completion.
- On phones (max-width 560px) Slate shows a bottom dock (Today, Home, Next, Calendar); Settings, Kids, Week, Stats and Add sit in the top row. `slate.bg` (Sky/Ocean video background) is device-only and not synced or backed up. The month calendar zooms to 2-3 weeks on pinch (`monthZoom`, not persisted).
- The app icon has three options (Blue by default, White, Black), chosen in Settings and stored device-only in `slate.icon`. The page points the `icon` and `apple-touch-icon` links at `favicon-<name>.png` and `apple-touch-icon-<name>.png`; iOS reads the icon when Slate is added to the Home Screen. Icons must stay square and opaque (iOS paints transparent pixels black). `icon-192/512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `favicon.png` are copies of the Blue set.
- iOS 26 bug: a home-screen app with the `black-translucent` status bar gets a page one status bar shorter than the screen (e.g. 797 of 844), leaving a strip at the bottom. Fixed layers cannot draw there, so when `navigator.standalone` and that gap exist, `html.fullh` makes html/body the full screen height (`--full-h`) and turns the full-screen layers (`#splash`, `.sheet-wrap`, `.dock`, `.ideas-page`, `.side-panel`, `#bgLayer`, `.hint`, `.month-foot`) into `position: absolute` on the body. The page is kept scrolled to the top. Set early in the head (tools/build.py) and kept up to date in `src/slate.html`.
- A fresh open lands on Home (unless the page reloaded inside the same visit via `slate.quick`); `slate.view` is still saved. The loading ring and its percent/"loading..." text are one centered group (`.ld-group`). The selected dock button lifts 9px. New profile pictures go through a drag/pinch/zoom round crop (`#cropSheet`) before being saved to `slate.avatar` as a 240px JPEG.
- Weekly routines (`__weekly` rules, tasks carry `rid`) stay until removed: filled 12 weeks ahead on open and extended (`ensureWeekly`) when you browse further ahead. Clearing a weekly task's text asks "Just this day / Every week"; "Every week" ends the rule and removes this and later days. Clear calendar also empties `__weekly`.
- iPad windows (iOS 26, standalone, `safeTop` 0) leave a strip above the page that the page cannot draw on; it shows `theme-color` / the html background. In the Sky/Ocean looks `sampleEdge()` averages the top edge of the background (with the `#bgLayer::after` tint) into `--edge`, used for `html[data-bg]` and the `theme-color` meta so that strip blends in. Do not turn on `html.fullh` for it (the gap is at the top, not the bottom). Section titles (`.h-label`) are forced white in the glass looks.
- `mac/` builds the Mac app (not part of the web app or Pages deploy): `main.js` is an Electron window around https://loriellecode.github.io/slate-app/ (so it always runs the latest Slate and shares nothing with other devices except through the account). `mac/build_mac.py` assembles Slate.app from Electron's prebuilt zips (`/tmp/el/el-{arm64,x64}.zip` from github.com/electron/electron releases, v33.2.1) and ad-hoc signs it with `rcodesign` (needed on Apple Silicon). It is unsigned by Apple, so the first open needs right-click > Open. npm cannot reach the registry from the sandbox unless `NO_PROXY=` is cleared; curl to github.com works.

- **Work from Workspace** (`public.work_items`, created by `supabase/work_items.sql`; the script is re-runnable and upgrades older tables in place): the Workspace app (loriellecode's creative studio) signs in to the same Slate account and sends tasks, calendar events and goals with `upsert_work_items`. Kinds: `task`, `event`, `goal` (one row per goal, `goal_id` = Workspace goal id) and `step` (a goal's tasks, ordered by `position`, grouped by `section` = milestone). Slate reads its own rows with `GET /rest/v1/work_items?updated_at=gt.<cursor>`, ticks them with `set_work_done(p_wid, p_done, p_at)`, and when every step of a goal is done it marks the goal row done too (Workspace then shows "“<goal>” goal was completed on iPad"). Work events added in Slate go through `add_work_event` (wid `slate:<random>`, `origin = 'slate'`); Workspace imports them and from then on owns them like its own. Workspace owns title/date/notes; completion is newest-change-wins (`done_changed_at`). Rows are cached device-only in `slate.work` (not in `slate.v1`, not synced, not backed up).
  - The suitcase button (`#workBtn`, next to Home) opens the Work page (`#workPage`) with two layouts for a goal, chosen by tabs and kept in `slate.worktab`: **Queue** (progress bar, "Now · i of n" card with Done, Up next, Done) and **Outline** (numbered milestones and numbered steps with checks). Loose tasks sent from Workspace are listed under "Other tasks from Workspace". Home has a Work card (`#hWork`).
  - Work events show only on the month calendar (both month builders add `workOn(key)`), never on the day list. The month view's "Show Work Schedule" switch (`#workCalBtn`, `slate.workcal`, on by default) hides or shows them; "+ Work event" (`#workAddBtn`, `#workEventSheet`) adds one. Pulls happen on open, when Slate comes back to the front, every minute, and from Refresh. Deleted items (`deleted = true`) are hidden.
